Do's
•	Keep your professional profiles accurate and up to date.
•	Think before you post.
•	Use privacy settings to control who can view your personal posts and information.
•	Engage positively by participating in academic discussions.
•	Credit original creators when sharing someone else's work.

Don'ts
•	Do not share personal information publicly.
•	Do not post offensive, discriminatory, or emotionally charged content.
•	Do not connect with unknown accounts offering jobs, prizes, or opportunities without verifying them first.
•	Do not mix your professional and personal tone.
•	Do not ignore suspicious activity on your accounts.
