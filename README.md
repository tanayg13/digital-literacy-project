## Project Overview

This repository contains all the work completed as part of the Digital Literacy project under VITyarthi.
The project is built around the role of a Student Digital Ambassador, where the goal is not just to use
digital tools but to understand them responsibly.

The project covers five key areas of digital literacy:
- Creating digital awareness resources
- Building a professional online presence
- Practising coding and collaboration platforms
- Communicating professionally in digital spaces
- Understanding cybercrime and staying safe online

## Module Summaries

### Task 1 — Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic
Created a one-page visual infographic covering what digital literacy is, useful
digital tools for students, safe internet practices, and email etiquette.

### Task 2 — Student Digital Portfolio
Set up and updated profiles on GitHub, LinkedIn, and Stack Overflow. Created a
GitHub profile README, filled in the LinkedIn Education section, created a Stack
Overflow profie and documented the purpose of each platform and future plans for
using them over the next four years.

### Task 3 — Coding and Collaboration Platforms
Used HackerRank to practise Python programming. Solved thebeginner-level
challenge "Solve Me First".
Made a Google form  that could be used as a 'Digital  Literacy Awareness
Quiz' for your batchmates.
Google form link: https://forms.gle/T1JTwb39CGtRtkoX8

### Task 4 — Professional Email & Etiquette Guide
Studied professional digital communication and wrote two formal emails — one
requesting an assignment extension from a professor, and one expressing interest
in a summer internship opportunity.
Made a Do's and Don'ts checklist for responsible social media use as a college student.

### Task 5 — Cybercrime Awareness Case Study & Prevention Guide
Researched UPI/Online Payment Fraud as the chosen cybercrime type. Wrote a
case study based on a real published incident.
Created a 9-point Stay Safe Online prevention checklist with specific UPI
safety tips and reporting channels including Helpline 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in.
