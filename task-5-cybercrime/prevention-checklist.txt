Stay Safe Online — Prevention Checklist
For College Students in India
=======================================

• Use a strong, unique password for every account and never reuse it across platforms.
• Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all important accounts.
• Never click on links from unknown senders received via SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
• Always check that a website URL starts with https:// before entering any personal details.
• Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking, UPI payments, or logging into sensitive accounts.
• Keep your phone, apps, and antivirus software updated at all times.
• Never enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money — you only need your PIN to send money.
• Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, CVV, or bank details with anyone, including bank helpline callers.
• Always verify the recipient's UPI ID or QR code carefully before confirming a payment.
• Review your social media privacy settings and limit what strangers can see about you.
